Catholic Calendar (US)

Roman Rite, USCCB proper calendar. Updates itself once you subscribe. Rebuilt monthly, always covering last year plus the next four.

Essentials

Solemnities, feasts, Sundays, Ash Wednesday, Holy Week, Triduum, US observances, seasons.

Full

Everything above plus every memorial and optional memorial.

How to subscribe

  1. Google Calendar: click "Google", or on calendar.google.com go to Other calendars → + → From URL and paste the link.
  2. Outlook: click "Outlook", or Add calendar → Subscribe from web and paste the link.
  3. Apple / iPhone: click "Apple".

Google refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours to a day; Outlook every few hours.
